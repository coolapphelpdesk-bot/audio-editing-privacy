Privacy Policy

Voice Recorder Audio Editor · last updated November 10, 2026

1. Summary

Voice Recorder Audio Editor (“the app”) is an audio recorder and editor published by TeamCoolApps. It records, edits, converts, and exports audio files.

  • Your audio recordings and edited files are processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • The app contains non-intrusive advertising via Google AdMob.
  • The app does not collect personal data beyond the advertising ID collected by AdMob.
  • Only two things ever reach the developer, and only when you choose to send them: a feedback message you write, and an optional anonymous vote for your favourite app theme.
  • If you use Find online in the Tags & cover tool, the words you type are sent to the MusicBrainz music database, only when you tap Search. Song titles and artists are sent to the LRCLIB lyrics database when you search for lyrics, or when you pick a song in Find online and the lyrics box is empty.

5. Network activity and third parties

All audio recording and editing happens on-device. The app integrates Google AdMob for non-intrusive advertising. AdMob may collect device information, advertising IDs, and IP addresses to serve ads. This data is managed by Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or attribution SDKs. All other libraries operate entirely on-device and do not transmit data.

Feedback and theme votes you send go over HTTPS to the developer’s own feedback service, hosted on Google Cloud. They are used only to reply to you and improve the app, and are never sold or shared.

Find online (Tags & cover tool) sends only the words you type to MusicBrainz, an open music database run by the non-profit MetaBrainz Foundation, and downloads cover pictures from the Cover Art Archive (hosted by the Internet Archive). Like any website, these services see your IP address. The search boxes start with words from the song’s tags or file name, which you can change before searching; no audio and no identifiers are sent, and nothing is sent until you tap Search. The optional “Search for a picture in your browser” button opens a Google image search in your browser, under Google’s own privacy policy.

Find lyrics (Tags & cover tool) sends only a song title and artist to LRCLIB, an open lyrics database, when you tap Search. Picking a song in Find online sends that song’s title and artist in the same way if the lyrics box is empty. Like any website, LRCLIB sees your IP address. No audio and no identifiers are sent. The optional “Search for the lyrics in your browser” button opens a Google search in your browser, under Google’s own privacy policy.

When you share an audio file via the system share sheet, the app passes a local file URI to the app you choose. The developer does not see what you share.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data you create in Voice Recorder Audio Editor remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Voice Recorder Audio Editor → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Recordings and exported files can be deleted manually through your device’s file manager.

Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to answer and act on them. Theme votes are anonymous and cannot be linked back to you. To have feedback you sent deleted, email the address below.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. Feedback and votes are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
